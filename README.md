**UC San Diego Jail Outreach Program Curricula**

This repository contains curricula for the following courses: Plant Biology and Introduction to Psychology. 

Both of these courses were developed by members of the UC San Diego Jail Outreach program and taught at re-entry facilities in the 2025-2026 academic year. The folders for each course contain the slides presented to the students and the activity associated with each lesson. There is a list of materials and instructions for each activity so that it can be replicated.

In addition to course curricula, this repository contains a Pre-Class Survey, a Post-Class Survey, and a Weekly Student Feedback Form. The Pre-Class Survey and Post-Class Survey were developed in 2026 by members of the UC San Diego Jail Outreach program to better understand the impact instructors have on students and how we can further design our lessons and curricula to the benefit of our students. Students fill out the Weekly Student Feedback Form at the end of each class to ask questions and assess what they learned. This form provides instructors with the opportunity to recap information or connect course material to other scientific topics during the next class.

Finally, we have included a Guide to Teaching a Course in a Jail that describes how to best prepare for these classes. This document is frequently utilized by instructors in the UC San Diego Jail Outreach program, but its principles can be applied to communicating science with a range of audiences. Since hosting a trauma-informed workshop was an integral part of our Research!America Public Engagement grant, we have added a section to this document on fostering healthy, working relationships with students that pulls from the teachings in this workshop.
